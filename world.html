Voting blocs beyond the United States, starting with Canada. Click Canada to explore its blocs.

Canada: fifteen blocs of polling divisions from the 2015, 2019, 2021 and 2025 federal elections (a Dirichlet-multinomial mixture of party vote counts, all four elections aligned to the 2025 polling divisions), in a Canadian color scheme after ColorBrewer’s Paired: blues Conservative, reds Liberal, oranges NDP, teals Bloc Québécois, greens Green-leaning and progressive. Equal Earth projection (Šavrič, Patterson & Jenny, 2018): every country drawn at its true relative size; graticule every 15°. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m.
