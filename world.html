Countries with a fitted mixture (a Dirichlet-multinomial mixture of party vote counts, or for Switzerland a beta-binomial mixture of referendum votes) are drawn as their four-bloc (K = 4) solution, the largest bloc palest; click one for its bloc map at the chosen K and its PCA figure, or for Canada its full interactive panels. Grey: data gathered or maps made, no fit yet. The green scale grades the sub-national election records available, from our regional surveys of election data. Equal Earth projection (Šavrič, Patterson & Jenny, 2018): every country drawn at its true relative size; graticule every 15°. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m.
