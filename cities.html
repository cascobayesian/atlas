Cities inside a covered state show the state’s blocs, cut to the city. Canadian cities show Canada’s fifteen national blocs (2015–2025 federal elections), cut to the city, in Canada’s own color scheme. French cities each have their own ten blocs, fitted to the polling stations’ presidential first-round votes (2012–2022 for Paris, Marseille, Lyon and Nantes; 2017–2022 for Nice, Montpellier and Strasbourg). Vienna has its own ten blocs, fitted jointly to its polling districts’ Gemeinderat (2015–2025) and Nationalrat (2013–2024) votes; its population figures are eligible voters.
